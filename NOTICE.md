# Attribution and terms

This repository contains the `plugin_analytics` and `bm_analytics` cartridges developed by Vedesh Reddy for Salesforce Storefront Reference Architecture (SFRA) storefronts. They are an SFRA extension, not an official Salesforce product.

The storefront cartridge extends SFRA controllers (listed in [docs/analytics-architecture.md](docs/analytics-architecture.md)) with `module.superModule` and `server.append`, implements the `app.template.afterFooter` hook, and uses SFRA's `server` module and middleware. The applicable Salesforce terms are preserved in [SFRA-TERMS.txt](licenses/SFRA-TERMS.txt).
